04 / METHODOLOGY & LIMITATIONSOPEN PROTOTYPE

The model

Baseline = monthly kWh × weighted peak/off-peak rates. Proposed scenario moves the selected portion of peak usage to off-peak rates while conserving total energy. It does not model tariff fees, taxes, storage losses, demand charges, equipment costs or dynamic wholesale prices.

What is real

The controls, calculation, scenario comparison, policy classifications and local draft run in the browser. Every result follows the actual editable inputs. No personal information leaves the device and no authentication is required.

What is proposed

Future integration with the separate Unfire Power Energy Prices app, verified local tariffs, optional consent-driven recommendations and the broader BOLT control layer. Those are a product direction, not features implemented here.
